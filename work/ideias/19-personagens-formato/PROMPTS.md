## Prompt-base (igual para os 12)
Edit the attached illustration. Keep EXACTLY the same drawing style: flat line art, a single solid navy ink color (#1F2A4E) on a fully transparent background,
solid filled hair, clean uniform outline of the same thickness, simple eyebrows, ears and neck lines, no shading, no gray, no gradients, no extra colors,
front view, same framing and proportions as the original (portrait, about 272 × 400). Keep the same hairstyle and gender.
Change only three things: (1) redraw the face outline so the face shape is anatomically precise for the shape described below;
(2) add eyeglasses drawn in the same navy line, clear lenses (no fill), frame style described below, sitting naturally on the nose and ears;
(3) add a mouth with a subtle, confident closed-lip smile — one simple curved line, calm and elegant, not cartoonish.
No text, no logo, no background, no border. Output a PNG with transparency.

## Linha de cada formato (colar depois do prompt-base)
- Oval: Face shape OVAL: face length about 1.5 times its width, widest at the cheekbones, gently rounded jawline and chin, balanced forehead. Glasses: classic soft-rectangle acetate frame, medium size (oval faces suit almost any frame).
- Redondo: Face shape ROUND: face length about equal to its width, full soft cheeks, rounded jaw with no angles, short rounded chin. Glasses: rectangular, angular frame, slightly wider than the face's cheek line, to add structure.
- Quadrado: Face shape SQUARE: forehead, cheekbones and jaw about the same width, strong straight angular jawline, flat wide chin. Glasses: round or oval frame to soften the angles.
- Alongado: Face shape OBLONG: face clearly longer than wide, straight cheek line from temple to jaw, long chin, tall forehead. Glasses: large, wide frame with tall lenses and a strong bridge to shorten the face visually.
- Coração: Face shape HEART: wide forehead and cheekbones tapering to a narrow, slightly pointed chin. Glasses: light thin-metal aviator frame (or thin rimless), bottom-heavy lenses to balance the chin.
- Diamante: Face shape DIAMOND: narrow forehead and narrow chin, cheekbones clearly the widest point, angular cheek line. Glasses: cat-eye frame for the woman / soft oval frame for the man, to highlight the cheekbones.
